Confidential Sources

Leak to
Y-O-X

Your courage matters. Your safety matters more. Y-O-X protects every source who comes to us — unconditionally and without exception.

Before you contact us

01

Use a personal device on a non-work network

Never contact us from a work computer, office WiFi, or any device your employer manages. Use your personal phone or laptop on a home or public network.

02

For highest anonymity, use Tor Browser

Download Tor Browser from torproject.org. It routes your connection through multiple servers, making it very difficult to trace. Recommended for high-risk situations.

03

Be mindful of document metadata

Documents often contain hidden data — author names, edit history, printer identifiers. If possible, photograph physical documents with a personal camera rather than scanning them on a work device.

04

Consider a disposable phone or SIM card

For high-risk situations, purchase a prepaid SIM with cash from a shop that doesn't require ID. Use it only for contacting Y-O-X, from a location away from your home or workplace. Never connect it to your regular accounts or WiFi networks you use daily. Once done, discard it.

05

No method is 100% secure

Any digital communication carries some risk. If you face serious legal or physical danger, consider consulting a lawyer before contacting us. We will never knowingly put a source at risk.

How to reach us

Signal

Recommended

End-to-end encrypted, no metadata stored. The most secure and accessible channel for most sources. Messages and files can be set to disappear automatically.

@yoxnews.01

ProtonMail

For large document transfers. Create a new ProtonMail account not linked to your identity. Send from that account to ours — end-to-end encrypted between ProtonMail accounts.

tips@yox-news.proton.me

Telegram

Secret Chat only

Only Telegram's Secret Chat mode is end-to-end encrypted — regular chats are not. Start a Secret Chat, enable auto-delete, and do not use Telegram Web. Suitable for initial contact; use Signal for document transfer.

@yoxnews

Physical mail or in-person

For the most sensitive material, physical delivery leaves no digital trace. Send to our editorial address using a public postbox, without a return address. For in-person meetings, contact us first via Signal to arrange.

Do not use Discord, WhatsApp, or regular email for sensitive tips. Discord stores all messages on its servers indefinitely. WhatsApp's metadata is shared with Meta. Regular email is not encrypted in transit.

Send a non-sensitive tip

For tips that do not put you at risk. This form is not encrypted — do not use it for sensitive material. Use Signal or ProtonMail for anything that could identify you.

Tip received

Our editorial team will review it. We'll follow up via Signal or ProtonMail if you provided contact details.

Our commitment to you

We will never

  • —Reveal your identity without explicit consent
  • —Cooperate with law enforcement against a source
  • —Publish material that could identify you
  • —Store your contact details longer than necessary
